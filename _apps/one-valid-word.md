## About the game

Swap letters until the word clicks. Every puzzle is a word whose letters spell no other common word, so there's always exactly one right answer. Everyone gets the same six-letter Daily and four-letter Daily Mini, each with a par, plus Free Play, interactive widgets and Game Center. Free with no ads; One Valid Word Plus is an optional one-time purchase. For iPhone and iPad. More at [onevalidword.com](https://onevalidword.com).
